## 3. Discovery

### 3.1 Classifier filename fast-path

`_rvClassifyType()` UNKNOWN cases → filename pattern fast-path
(`^SA_(일별|성별|연령|시간대별|검색어)` → kind='SA').
False-positive 0 (explicit prefix). Resolved 4 SA reload paths.

### 3.2 Workbook cell cap silent overflow (NEW)

SA_시간대별 reload (93,337 rows × 25 cols ≈ 2.33 M cells) **attempted against
workbook with current usage 9.28 M cells** (V20 G2c² baseline 92.84% × 10 M
cap → 9,284,414 cells precise; remaining headroom 0.72 M).

→ Projected post-reload: 9.28 + 2.33 = **11.61 M > HARD_CAP 10 M**, blocked
with response message:

> "이 작업을 실행하면 통합문서의 셀 개수가 한도인 10000000개를 초과합니다"

**Different dimension from sheet maxRows cap** (Quirk #4 — per-sheet 100 K
rows). This is workbook-wide 10 M cells limit. β cleanup trim idempotent
(V19-B5 cleanup 후 추가 회수 0) confirmed cleanup is not a viable recovery
path for this case.

→ Promoted to APPENDIX #8.

### 3.3 verify false-positive (NEW)

`verifyDedupBatch()` for SA_시간대별 (dataRows=0 after cell-cap block) returned
ok=true (multi-dupe 검사 대상 없음). requireNonEmpty guard absent.

→ Promoted to APPENDIX #9.

### 3.4 Cleanup idempotent contract (POSITIVE)

`trimEmptyRowsCols()` 2nd run: recovered=0, trims=0.
**Invariant**: idempotent guarantee (not failure).
Critical to label as `contract` not `quirk` to prevent
"this is a limitation, avoid" misreading.

→ Promoted to APPENDIX #10 (contract label).
